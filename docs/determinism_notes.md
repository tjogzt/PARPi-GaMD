# Determinism notes (for reviewer queries)

Properties observed on the frozen release (clean-copy chain runs, 2026-10-06).

## Bit-level determinism (verified)

- **S2/S1 consolidated table** (`s2_dbe_final.csv`): clean-copy chain vs
  shipped tree — maximum absolute difference **0.0** across all 30 rows.
- **Reweighted well-depth archive** (`s2_dbe_well_depths.npy`): deep-compared
  identical (nested walk, zero differences).
- **S2 PMF xvg curves**: 48/48 byte-identical between a clean-copy chain and
  the shipped tree; the reconstruction-trio C1/C2 curves are regenerated
  deterministically by the same chain step.
- **Figures**: every regenerated figure is pixel-identical at 100 dpi to the
  shipped copy; PDF byte differences, when present, are Cairo writer
  metadata only.
- **PDF text layers** (manifest verification): stable across regeneration
  (the `s10` checker's PDF convention is the `pdftotext` digest).

## Last-digit jitter (documented; no material impact)

- **DCCM products**: some entries reproduce with ~1e-5-level floating-point
  jitter in the last stored digit; means, signed means and the r > 0.3 /
  r < −0.3 fractions are unaffected (differences below reported precision).
- **Dihedral-group energy CSVs**: frame-level values agree to the displayed
  precision; platform-dependent floating-point summation may differ in last
  digits (OpenMM/OpenCL environment note from the audit, W-C).

## Non-distributed intermediates (explicitly annotated)

- `data/01_modeling/docking_ligands/` (s01 RDKit outputs; RDKit-version
  dependent) — intermediates, not shipped in the repository.
- `data/analysis/sys1_*_{cv,weights}.dat` and `results/dihed_*.csv` — chain
  intermediates regenerated by `rebuild_all.sh` steps [4]/[6]; the
  reconstructed trio's C1/C2 xvg curves are likewise chain-regenerated.

## Hash-order independence

- No shipped helper depends on set/dict iteration order for its numerical
  output (checked mechanically); `rebuild_all.sh` nevertheless exports
  `PYTHONHASHSEED=0` as a defensive measure.

## Kabsch overflow warning (`code/45_talazoparib_rmsf_control.py`)

Reported during the audit (RuntimeWarning, matmul). **Verified spurious — no
numerical impact.** Minimal reproduction: `docs/code_audit_20261005/
repro_kabsch_overflow.py` scanned all 26,000 frames of the talazoparib S2
trajectory (kabsch_align on CA coordinates): every frame raises the
"divide by zero / overflow / invalid encountered in matmul" trio while the
aligned output stays finite and the coordinates stay physical
(global max |coord| = 158.7 Å). A further minimal probe — pure
`A.T @ B` on Gaussian random matrices under `warnings.simplefilter("always")`
— raises the identical trio with a finite result, whereas `np.einsum` on
the same operands raises nothing. `numpy.show_config()` reports the build
linked against **Apple Accelerate** BLAS (aarch64): the warnings are an
Accelerate framework artifact, independent of the data. Conclusion: annotate
only (header note in `code/45`); no code change.
